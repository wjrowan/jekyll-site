## Overview

This guide explains how to carry over any unresolved minutes and agenda items from a previous meeting into the current one.

## Starting the Meeting

[Create a meeting](create%20a%20meeting) and/or [open one](open%20a%20meeting) that is scheduled to occur soon, where you intend to carry over pending actions and motions.

## Carrying Over Pending Actions and Motions from a Previous Meeting

Open the special agenda item titled ```Confirmation of the minutes of the previous general meeting``` (by default, this is the 3rd agenda item).

![Image](../media\meetings\prepare_an_agenda_and_carry_over_minutes\Prepare_an_agenda_and_carry_over_minutes_1.png)

Click the "Include" button to automatically import all unresolved motions and actions into the current meeting for discussion or reassignment.

### Common Issues and Solutions

**Nothing Carried Over**: This indicates that all actions and motions from the previous meeting have been resolved, and there is nothing pending.

**Cannot Find the Agenda Item**: The default meeting format may have been customized by an admin in the settings. As a result, the location of this item may vary depending on your configuration.
